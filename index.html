About Giskard

Giskard (GSK) is a WorldChain token with a single, powerful premise: one token for every human being on Earth. The total supply of 8,161,972,572 GSK mirrors the current global population figure from the United Nations World Population Prospects — making it one of the few tokens with genuine real-world anchoring.

Built on WorldChain (Chain ID: 480) — the blockchain ecosystem designed for the Worldcoin/World ID network — GSK is positioned at the intersection of human identity, decentralized finance, and AI-powered futures. WorldChain provides near-zero gas fees and native human verification.

GSK is tradeable on Uniswap V3 against both WLD and USDC.e, with 1% fee pools providing liquidity. The contract is non-upgradeable and permissionless — the supply is fixed and the oracle is transparent.
